Good to know

Event basics

The short version: All Team Bash is about community first. Come ready for cars, vendors, food, games, music, and a whole lot of people who love the lifestyle.

About ATB

What is All Team Bash?

All Team Bash is an automotive lifestyle event hosted by Kimo & Maria. It was created to bring teams, enthusiasts, families, builders, vendors, and creatives together without making the day all about judging or awards.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Is it family friendly?

Yes. ATB is made for enthusiasts and their families, so bring the crew and enjoy the day together.

🎟️

How much is entry?

Entry is typically $20 at the gate or $15 online during presale. Kids 10 and under get in free. Military and first responder discounts may be available with valid ID.

📲

Where do I buy tickets?

Presale links are usually posted on the schedule page and on the Facebook event page. You can also pay at the gate unless an event says otherwise.

⏰

What time does it run?

Most ATB events are open to the public from 9 AM to 2 PM. Always check the schedule page for the exact event details before you head out.

🚘

Do I need to be on a team?

Nope. You do not have to be part of a team or car club. If you support the scene, you are welcome.

At the event

Bring the vibe

A few practical things to know so the day stays easy, respectful, and fun for everyone.

🐶

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Dogs are welcome as long as they stay on a leash. Please clean up after them and be mindful of the crowd, heat, and noise.

🍔

Can I bring food or drinks?

Yes. Food, BBQ, and ice chests are allowed. Just keep your setup clean and make sure charcoal is completely cooled before disposal.

🛍️

Will there be vendors?

Yes. ATB works to bring food trucks, local vendors, small businesses, merch, and event extras to create a full day — not just a parking lot meetup.

🏆

Are there trophies?

ATB does not judge cars for trophy categories. Instead, trophies may be awarded through games, and the King of Clubs specialty trophy is passed from team to team.

Vendors, media & merch

Want to vend, shoot media, or grab ATB gear? Here is the easiest way to get pointed in the right direction.

Vendors

Want a booth or food truck spot?

Email allteambash@gmail.com with the event you are interested in and a description of your business. Vendor approval and pricing may vary by event.

Media

Want a media pass?

ATB offers a limited number of media passes for each event. Email the team, name the event, and include a quick description of who you are and what you shoot.

Merch

Can I buy ATB merch?

Yes. The merchandise booth usually opens at 10 AM unless otherwise posted. Items often include shirts, hats, decals, lanyards, and more.
